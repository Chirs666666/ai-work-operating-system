# Diagnosis Framework
Evaluate 12 dimensions: URL Ownership Alignment, Search Intent Alignment, Business/ICP Alignment, Page-Type Fit, Offer Clarity, Content Coverage, Technical/Buyer Specificity, Evidence/Trust, Differentiation, Conversion Path, Information Architecture/Section Flow, Existing Performance Preservation Risk.
States: PASS, WARN, FAIL, UNKNOWN.
Diagnosis must separate page-content problems from metadata, internal-link, technical, speed, and ownership problems.
