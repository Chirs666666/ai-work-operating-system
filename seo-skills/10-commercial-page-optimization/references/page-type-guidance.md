# Page-Type Guidance
PRODUCT: product capability, specs, fit, applications, evidence, buying path.
CATEGORY: product family differentiation, selection logic, child-page routing.
SERVICE: service scope, process, capability boundaries, evidence, inquiry path.
APPLICATION: problem/process fit, environment, technical requirements, outcomes with evidence.
INDUSTRY: sector requirements, applicable capabilities, standards/evidence where verified.
CAPABILITY: manufacturing/process competence, limits, equipment/process proof.
LANDING_PAGE: focused offer/intent and conversion path; avoid unsupported urgency.
HOME commercial modules: clarify positioning, priority offers, proof, navigation and conversion; do not redesign the whole site without 04.
