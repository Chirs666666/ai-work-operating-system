# Boundaries and Ownership
12 owns technical SEO diagnosis and remediation decisions. 25 owns keyword/topic-to-URL ownership. 08 owns semantic internal-link architecture. 21 owns page speed/CWV. 19 owns post-fix index/ranking lifecycle. 09 owns Title/Meta. 06/10 own content. 07 may be the execution owner for WordPress page-body changes.
A canonical, redirect, or noindex recommendation must not silently redefine ownership.
