# Source Classification

- `USER_PROVIDED`: material supplied/confirmed by the customer or authorized user.
- `COMPANY_OFFICIAL`: company-controlled website, catalog, datasheet, announcement, or approved corporate material.
- `EXTERNAL_PRIMARY`: authoritative original external record, e.g. standards/certification body or original technical manufacturer source.
- `EXTERNAL_SECONDARY`: third-party reporting, directories, articles, aggregators, or summaries.
- `INFERRED`: model/human inference not directly stated by a source. It is not evidence.
- `UNKNOWN`: provenance cannot be established.

Always record source identity/location and date/version when known. External sources may challenge or corroborate a customer fact but must not silently overwrite it.
