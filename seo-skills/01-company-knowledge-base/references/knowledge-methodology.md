# Knowledge Methodology

Use this sequence: source inventory → entity extraction → company facts → product facts → capability separation → evidence mapping → conflict detection → terminology normalization → claims classification → knowledge-gap analysis → current-task sufficiency → closeout.

## Extraction rules
Preserve original notation for technical facts when useful. Attach evidence references to important facts. Do not merge models because their names look similar. Separate what a source states from what has been independently verified.

## Sufficiency
Judge readiness against the requested downstream task, not an imaginary requirement for a perfectly complete company database. Missing non-critical data yields WARN; missing facts essential to the requested task yield BLOCK.
