# Evidence Levels

- **E1 — Primary Verified:** approved engineering/specification record, certificate, formal first-party record, or explicit authorized confirmation appropriate to the claim.
- **E2 — Company Official:** official company website/catalog/marketing material that supports what the company states.
- **E3 — Strong External:** authoritative technical/standards/manufacturer source that corroborates or defines the fact.
- **E4 — Secondary / Reported:** directory, third-party article, repost, search result, or other secondary report. It proves that a source reported the claim, not necessarily that the claim is true.
- **E5 — Unsupported:** no traceable supporting source, model inference, or unsupported marketing assertion.

E5 cannot support publication as fact. Evidence level is not identical to publication permission; sensitive/customer-confidential E1 material may still be prohibited from public use.
