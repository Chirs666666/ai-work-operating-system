# Claims Policy

## GREEN
Publishable when the claim is supported and publication is allowed. Example: a product repeatability value supported by an approved specification sheet.

## YELLOW
Conditional. Use only with the required context, qualifier, benchmark, scope, or attribution. Examples: “reduces programming time” without a universal baseline; payback or productivity observations limited to a documented scenario.

## RED
Do not publish without explicit approval and adequate evidence. Examples include unsupported superlatives (“best manufacturer”), guaranteed ROI/performance, unapproved customer names, unverified certifications, or confidential claims.

Never upgrade YELLOW/RED to GREEN merely because the wording is persuasive.
