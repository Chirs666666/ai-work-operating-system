# Conflict Resolution

When two credible sources disagree, retain both candidate values with source provenance, date/version, affected entity/field, severity, and downstream impact. Mark `CONFLICT` and `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` where applicable.

Do not silently choose a winner for critical specifications, certifications, dates, customer results, dimensions, tolerances, payloads, performance, or legal/company identity. A newer source may be a resolution only when its authority/scope clearly supersedes the old source; preserve the prior value in history.

Critical unresolved conflict → BLOCK dependent work. Non-critical conflict → WARN and isolate the affected claim.
