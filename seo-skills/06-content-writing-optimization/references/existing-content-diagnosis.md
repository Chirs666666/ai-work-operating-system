# Existing Content Diagnosis
Required before UPDATE/REFRESH/REWRITE. Review current article, query/page evidence when available, current SERP, freshness, evidence quality, intent match, coverage, Information Gain, readability, repetition, and link context.
Output what works, what is stale/missing/incorrect, what should be preserved, and justified change depth. Performance data is preferred but absence alone does not fabricate a BLOCK; record availability and confidence.
