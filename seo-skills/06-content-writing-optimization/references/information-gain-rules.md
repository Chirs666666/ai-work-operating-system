# Information Gain
Document: SERP Baseline, Content Gap, Decision Gap, Evidence Gap, verified Company Experience where relevant, and an Information Gain Plan.
Gain may be boundary conditions, decision rules, validation methods, synthesis of authoritative sources, examples, checklists, clearer frameworks, or verified experience. “Longer than competitors” is not gain. CREATE/REFRESH/REWRITE require an explicit assessment; if no meaningful added value is possible, G06 cannot PASS merely because a draft is longer.
