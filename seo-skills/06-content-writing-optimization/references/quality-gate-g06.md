# G06 Content Readiness
PASS: applicable content requirements met.
WARN: non-critical documented gaps; safe downstream work may continue.
FAIL: material content defect requires 06 revision.
BLOCK: critical evidence/input/ownership decision missing.
REUSE: existing content remains adequate; no justified edit.
QA observations distinguish PASS/FAIL from NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_CHECKED, UNKNOWN. G06 never asserts indexation, ranking, traffic, inquiries, or GEO visibility.
