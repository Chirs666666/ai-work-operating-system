# Evidence & Claim Policy
Channel A — Company Claims: use 01 Company Knowledge Base or approved USER_DATA. Channel B — Topic Knowledge: USER_DATA, API_DATA, PUBLIC_RESEARCH and authoritative external sources.
Knowledge classes: COMPANY_FACT, EXTERNAL_FACT, SUPPORTED_EXPLANATION, EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT, UNKNOWN.
Track material claims: numbers, formulas, standards, safety/compliance, performance, important comparisons, company capabilities, and consequential technical judgments. Every sentence does not need a claim record. Preserve conflicts. Missing evidence is UNKNOWN/NOT_PROVIDED.
