# Mode Selection
CREATE: no current informational article exists for the confirmed ownership.
UPDATE: intent and most useful content remain valid; changes are localized.
REFRESH: intent remains valid but coverage, evidence, structure, freshness, or Information Gain needs systematic improvement.
REWRITE: current body materially fails confirmed intent and major restructuring is justified.
REUSE is a gate outcome for existing content when no justified edit exists. REWRITE cannot solve URL ownership or consolidation problems; route those to 25.
