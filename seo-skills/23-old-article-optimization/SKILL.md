# 1. Purpose
Decide whether an existing article should be kept, monitored, refreshed, expanded, rewritten, merged, repositioned or supported by metadata/internal links/authority/technical work.

# 2. Trigger
Use for published informational articles with declining, stagnant, outdated, thin, drifting, cannibalized, weak-CTR or conversion concerns.

# 3. Do Not Trigger
Do not rewrite merely because traffic fell or the article is old. Do not merge from keyword similarity alone. Do not infer no Google ranking from Semrush absence. Do not casually change a backlinked/ranking URL.

# 4. Inputs
Current article, URL/index/canonical status, GSC/GA4/Semrush/Ahrefs evidence, historical performance, SERP observations, ownership mapping, internal/backlink evidence and business goal.

# 5. Preconditions
Preserve source provenance and historical equity. If technical/index health is unresolved, BLOCK content diagnosis where it would invalidate conclusions.

# 6. Workflow
ARTICLE → HISTORY → QUERIES → OWNERSHIP → SERP INTENT → FRESHNESS/GAPS → LINKS/TRUST/CONVERSION → DIAGNOSIS → PRESERVATION → DECISION → BRIEF → HANDOFF → VERIFY.

# 7. Tool Routing
Use first-party data when available; third-party tools are complementary. 25 confirms ownership; 06 executes substantive informational rewriting; 20 validates ranking outcomes.

# 8. Decision Rules
Diagnose impression, position, CTR, demand, SERP intent, ownership, seasonality and technical factors before blaming content. Preserve proven sections/assets unless evidence supports changing them.

# 9. Quality Gates
G23: PASS, WARN, FAIL, BLOCK, REUSE, WAITING_APPROVAL.

# 10. Outputs
Use the 24 canonical outputs in references/canonical-outputs.md.

# 11. Handoff
06 content; 08 internal links; 09 metadata; 11 authority; 12 technical SEO; 20 ranking validation; 25 ownership/cannibalization.

# 12. Safety / Change Control
23 proposes changes. URL changes, redirects, merges, deletions, publishing and other live modifications remain approval-gated downstream actions.
